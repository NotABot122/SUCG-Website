Our Mission

Built on Purpose. Driven by People.

The Seattle University Consulting Group exists to provide students with hands-on exposure to real-world clients and engagements.

We equip students with the skills, experience, and industry connections needed to succeed in consulting and beyond.

Every project creates value for the student consultant and client organization.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Professionalism First

Students are selected and trained to operate at a professional standard. Every client interaction reflects that commitment — from first meeting to final presentation.

Scoped to Succeed

Clear scope means better work, lower risk, and a more productive engagement for everyone involved. We commit to what we can deliver — and deliver what we commit to.

Community Connection

Every engagement strengthens the ties between Seattle University and the regional business community, creating lasting value beyond any single project.

Structured Thinking

We bring a structured approach, analytical rigor, and evidence-backed insights to every challenge, delivering results with clarity and confidence.

Continuous Learning

We are students. Continuous learning is not just a value — it is how we operate. Every challenge is approached with a growth mindset and a willingness to adapt.

Accountability

We hold ourselves to the commitments we make. If we take on a project, we deliver — on time, within scope, and to a professional standard.
